Who We Are

Alumarvel — where metal and glass meet architecture

Alumarvel is a Saudi-based company specializing in providing engineering solutions in the fields of aluminum and glass, headquartered in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia.

We deliver high-quality works that comply with the project’s technical specifications and are tailored to meet its specific requirements and objectives. Our solutions are practical, efficient, and built upon our extensive experience and expertise in the industry. We are committed to delivering reliable results and providing warranties for the completed works, ensuring quality, performance, and customer satisfaction.

Our Services

What We Do

Two disciplines, one production line. We design, fabricate and install both the frame and the glass it holds.

01 — Frame & Structure

Aluminum Works

  • Aluminum Doors — Sliding, Hinged & Folding
  • Aluminum Canopies — Pergolas & Car Shades
  • Thermal & Non-Thermal Aluminum Windows
  • Aluminum Facades with High-Quality Finishing & Insulation
  • Decorative Metal Works & CNC
  • Modern & Heat-Resistant Canopies and Shades
02 — Clarity & Light

Glass Works

  • Double-Glazed Glass for Superior Thermal & Sound Insulation
  • Skylights for Natural Lighting & Strong Insulation
  • Glass Facades — Curtain Wall, Structural & Frameless
  • Glass Railings for Stairs & Balconies with Premium Finishing
