Who We Are

Alumarvel — where metal and glass meet architecture

Alumarvel is an integrated aluminum and glass manufacturer, founded in 2007 to close the gap between architectural drawings and buildable, weather-tight reality. We hold every stage — extrusion, CNC machining, coating, tempering and glazing — inside a single 40,000 m² facility.

That control means fewer handoffs, tighter tolerances, and a factory floor our engineers can walk before a project ever reaches site. Contractors and architects come to us for aluminum systems and glass that perform exactly as specified, on the schedule they were promised.
